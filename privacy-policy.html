2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and help you with your property requirement.

Information you give us

  • Contact details: your name and mobile number.
  • Requirement details: the property type, category, specification, location and budget you choose, and anything you write in the "budget and location" box.
  • Messages: the details you send us on WhatsApp, phone call or email, and any documents you choose to share with us for verification, registry or loan support.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type and pages opened, which your browser shares when a page loads, including from the third-party services listed in section 5.

We do not ask for passwords, bank card numbers or PINs through this website. Please do not send them to us.

4. Who we share it with

We share your information only when needed and only as described here:

  • Our team: our advisors who handle your enquiry.
  • Builders, developers and property owners: when you ask us to arrange a site visit, booking or negotiation for a specific property, we share the details needed for that purpose.
  • Banks and service providers: for home loan, documentation, legal or registry support, only when you ask for that service.
  • Technology providers: services that help us run the website and receive your enquiries (section 5).
  • Authorities: where the law requires us to, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

5. Third-party services we use

  • FormSubmit: when you submit the "Book a free consultation" form, your details are sent to our email through the FormSubmit service.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): our forms and buttons open WhatsApp with your message ready to send. Anything you send there is handled under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. The message is sent only when you tap Send.
  • Google Fonts: loads the fonts used on this website, which shares your IP address with Google.
  • Unsplash: some images on this website are loaded from Unsplash servers.
  • Social media: if you open our Facebook, Instagram or YouTube pages, those platforms apply their own policies.

These providers have their own privacy practices, and we encourage you to read them.

6. Cookies and similar tools

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking tools of its own. Your browser or the third-party services above may store small files or technical data to load content properly. You can control cookies from your browser settings. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this policy.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your information for as long as it is needed to deal with your enquiry, provide the services you asked for, keep business records, and meet legal requirements. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous. You can ask us to delete your data earlier (see section 9).

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. This website uses a secure connection (HTTPS). However, no method of transfer over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

9. Your rights and choices

In line with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw your consent, for example to stop calls and messages from us;
  • address a grievance about how your information is handled.

To use any of these rights, write to info@nathelrealtors.com or call 9319113716. We may need to confirm your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is functional under the law.

10. Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has shared information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Links to other websites

Our website may link to other websites, such as social media pages or builder websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the "Last updated" date at the top. Please check it now and then.
